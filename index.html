Questions

Do I need to be 21 to shop?

Yes. Haze N Blaze sells tobacco, vape, Delta products and adult novelties, so you must be 21 or older and show valid ID in store.

Can I order online or get delivery?

No — shopping is in-store only in Carrollton and Dallas. There’s no online checkout on this site. Call your store to ask about a product, then pick it up in person.

What’s the difference between the two stores?

Carrollton leans into American vapes, legal Delta products, cigars, cigarette & cigarillos, e-juice, pods & coils, grinders, scales, trays and adult novelties. Dallas is listed for CBD, Delta 8, tobacco, vape, kratom and hookah. Plenty overlaps — and promos like Geek Bar run in Carrollton and in Dallas.

Which store opens earlier?

Carrollton opens at 9:00 AM Monday–Saturday; Dallas opens at 9:30 AM. Each closes at 9:30 PM, and each opens 10 AM – 8 PM on Sundays. The live countdown at the top always uses Central Time.

How do I check if something is in stock?

Call the store: Carrollton (469) 289-0012 or Dallas (469) 372-0868. You can also watch each store’s Instagram — @hazenblaze2 for Carrollton and @hazenblazedallas for Dallas — for new drops.
